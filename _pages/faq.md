## Can I get ETCS credits for attending the winter school?
**Answer:** Probably. The winter school cannot grant ETCS cretids by itself, but we can provide a certificate of attendance with recommendations for credits that the student submit to their home institution, contingent on that the student hands in a written report on their learnings from thr school. The current recommendation is 5 ECTS credits. To the best of our knowledge, no student has been denied credits from their home institution after attendig the school.

## How do I get to Geilo?
**Answer:** Geilo is accessible from Oslo or Bergen by Train (approximately 3 hours scenic train route), see [Vy](https://www.vy.no/en/) for timetables and tickets. Oslo and Bergen are well connected by plane.
