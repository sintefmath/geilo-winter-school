Below, you will find answers to frequently asked questions. Do not hesitate to get in touch if you cannot find the one you are looking for.

## What does a typical winter school week look like?
A typical winter school day starts with lectures from 09:00 to 10:30, followed by a long midday break for lunch and socializing, whether you are skiing or enjoying the hotel spa facilities. Lectures resume at 15:00 and continue until 18:30, before a shared dinner in the hotel restaurant at 19:30. The image below shows a typical program for the full week.

## Can I get ECTS credits for attending the winter school?
Probably. The winter school itself cannot award ECTS credits, but we can provide a certificate of attendance with recommendations for credits that students can submit to their home institution. This is generally contingent on the student submitting a written report on their learning from the school. **The current recommendation is 5 ECTS credits**. To the best of our knowledge, no student has been denied credits from their home institution after attending the school.

## How do I get to Geilo?
Geilo is accessible from Oslo and Bergen by train, via an approximately 3-hour scenic route. See [Vy](https://www.vy.no/en/) for timetables and tickets. Oslo and Bergen are well connected by air.

## What is the weather like in Geilo in January?
January in Geilo can be very cold (previous years have seen temperatures down to -20°C / -4°F with strong winds). Please bring warm clothing, headwear, and sturdy footwear. See the updated weather forecast on [yr.no](https://www.yr.no/en/forecast/daily-table/1-112132/Norway/Buskerud/Hol/Geilo).

## How do I go skiing during the breaks?
For alpine skiing, you will need an existing ski pass or purchase one. The hotel typically offers discounted lift passes. For cross-country skiing, the tracks are open and free to use for everyone. For updated snow conditions, see [Skiinfo](https://www.skiinfo.no/ostlandet/geilo/snoerapport).

The hotel and the ski resort both offer equipment rental. **Please remember to bring appropriate clothing.**
